Teddy is a experimental physics researcher. For his PhD research, he conducted experiments to investigate the dielectric and crystallographic properties of novel, molecular crystals, designed and synthesized by chemists at the University of Colorado Boulder, the Czech Academy of Science, and the University of Milano-Bicocca. During this time, he prepared samples, grew thin films, and conducted a varity of experiments to characterize powders and thin films.
